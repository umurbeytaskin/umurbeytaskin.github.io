Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Write to umurbeytaskinn@gmail.com. Every message is read and answered by the developer. If something isn’t working, mention your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Sunstep. You can also get there from Sunstep: Settings, then Manage subscription.

If you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

I bought Sunstep Pro, but it isn’t active.

In Sunstep, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

My steps end without a sound.

Check that notifications are allowed: iPhone Settings, Notifications, Sunstep. If they are off, Sunstep also shows a notice on its home screen.

Silent mode and Focus can keep notifications quiet. Alarm mode rings like an alarm clock instead, even on silent. It needs iOS 26 or later and Sunstep Pro; turn it on with the bell button in the player and allow alarms when asked.

The countdown doesn’t appear on my Lock Screen.

Open iPhone Settings, then Apps, then Sunstep, and make sure Live Activities is on. The countdown appears the next time you start a routine.

Where is the Apple Watch app?

It installs with the iPhone app when automatic app install is on. If you don’t see it, open the Watch app on your iPhone, find Sunstep under My Watch, and tap Install. It needs watchOS 10 or later.

My routines don’t sync between devices.

Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on for Sunstep: iPhone Settings, your name, iCloud. New changes can take a few minutes to arrive. In Sunstep, Settings shows whether iCloud sync is on.

How do I change the language or the voice?

Sunstep follows your iPhone’s language. To use a different language just for Sunstep, open iPhone Settings, then Apps, Sunstep, Language. Voice guidance speaks in the app’s language, and you can turn it off in Sunstep’s Settings.

How do I delete my data?

Delete routines inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything on that device. To remove Sunstep’s copy in iCloud, open iPhone Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage, then Sunstep.
