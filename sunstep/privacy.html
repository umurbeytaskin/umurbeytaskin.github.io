Privacy Policy

Sunstep for iPhone and Apple Watch · Last updated 15 September 2026

The short version. Sunstep does not collect personal data. Your routines stay on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What Sunstep stores

Your routines, steps, run history and settings are stored on your device. Sunstep has no user accounts and no server of its own, and we cannot see your routines.

iCloud sync

If iCloud is turned on for Sunstep, your routines and run history are also kept in the private iCloud database of your own Apple Account, so they appear on your other devices. Apple stores and encrypts this data under its own privacy policy. It is not shared with us, and we have no way to read it. You can turn this off for Sunstep in iPhone Settings under your name, then iCloud.

Apple Watch

The watch app receives your routines and the routine that is running directly from your iPhone, using Apple’s WatchConnectivity. Nothing passes through us.

Siri, Shortcuts and voice guidance

If you start a routine with Siri or Shortcuts, Apple handles that request under its own privacy policy. Voice guidance uses the speech voices built into iOS. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

What Sunstep does not do

  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No advertising.
  • No collection of names, email addresses, contacts, location or health data.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.

Permissions

Notifications tell you when a step ends, when a routine reminder is due, and two days before a free trial ends. Alarms, used by the optional alarm mode on supported devices, ring a system alarm at the end of a step. Live Activities show the countdown on your Lock Screen. You can turn each of these off in iPhone Settings.

Purchases

Sunstep Pro is sold through Apple’s in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment, and Sunstep never receives your payment details. Your purchase status is checked on your device using Apple’s StoreKit.

Deleting your data

Delete routines inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything on that device. To remove Sunstep’s data from iCloud, open iPhone Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage, then Sunstep.

Children

Sunstep is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from the site itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub’s own privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
